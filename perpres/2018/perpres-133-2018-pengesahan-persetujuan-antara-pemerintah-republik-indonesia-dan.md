ARTICLE 1

Nationals of the Republlc of lndonesia and nationals of Ukraine, holding valid diplomatic and service passpcr-ts, shall not be required to obtain a visa to enter into, stay in and transit tirrough the territory of the State of tne other Party.

CONSULAR OFFICES

Nationals of the State of either Party, holding passports, referred to in Article 'l of this Agreement and assigned as members of the diplomatic mission, consular post or official mission of their State to international organizations, accredited in the territory of the State of the other Party, as well as their family nrembers who form paft of their household and are their dependants shall be required to obtain appropriate entry visa from the diplomatic mission or consular post of the receiving State prior to their arrival.

PASSPORT VALIDITY

The validity of passports. referred to in Article 1 of this Agreement shall be at least 6 (six) months actual on the date of entry of their holders into the territory of the State of either Par1y.

ARTICLE 5

ENTRY AND EXIT CONDITIONS

Nationals of the state of either Par1y, holding passports. referred to in Adicle 1 of this Agreement, shall enter into, exit from and transit through the territory of the State of the other Party through all international passenger crossing points.

RIGHTS OF AUTHORITIES

1. Nationals of the state of either par1y, holding passports, referred to in Article 1 of this Agreement, shall abide by appricable taws and regulations in force during their entry into, stay in, exit from and transit through the territory of the State of the other party.

DEPARTURE IN CASE OF LOST OR DAMAGED DOCUMENTS

1. Any national of the state of either party, who has lost his/her diplomatic or service passport or has had such passport damaged whrle staying in the territory of the State of the other party, shall immediately inform thereof the competent authorities of the receiving state, which, in this case, shail issue a certificate acknowledging the receipt of the application on the loss or damage of the said document.

ARTICLE B

SPECIMENS OF PASSPORTS

1. The Parties shall exchange, through the diplomatic channels, specimens of the passports, referred to in Article 1 of this Agreement, including a detailed description of such passports, within a period not less than 30 (thirty) days prior to the date of entry into force of this Agreement.

ENTRY INTO FORCE, DURATION AND TERMINATION

This Agreement shall enter into force after 30 (thirty) days from the date of receipt, through diplomatic channels, of the last written notification concerning the completion by the parties of their internal procedures required for this Agreement to enter into force and shall be valid for an indefinite period.

2. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, by sending written notification to the other Party through diplomatic channels. The termination shall take effect g0 (ninety) days after the date of receiving by the other Party of the said notification.
