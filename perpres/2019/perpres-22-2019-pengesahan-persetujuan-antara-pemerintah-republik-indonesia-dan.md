1. Any intellectual property acquired by one Party, its Executive Agencies or Cooperation Organizations while independently carrying out any work under this

Agreement shall be owned by that Party, its Executive Agencies or Cooperation

Organizations.

2. Any intellectual property acquired by the Parties, their Executive Agencies or Cooperation Organizations while jointly carrying out any work under this Agreement shall be jointly owned by the Parties, their Executive Agencies or

Coo peration Orga nizations.

3. The Parties, their Executive Agencies or Cooperation Organizations may enter into separate agreements regarding the use and protection procedures for the jointly owned intellectual property.

4. Neither party shall provide to any third party the joint work achievements without the written consent of the other Party.

ARTICLE 9

Amendment

This Agreement may be amended at any time by mutual consent of the Parties by means of letter exchange through diplomatic channel.
